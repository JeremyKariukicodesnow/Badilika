YOUR FIRST FEW MINUTES

One step at a time.
At your own pace.

Follow the steps you need. The app’s own setup guide is skippable and always available again in Settings → Setup guide.

01

Install or update.

Download the Android APK from this website and open it. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager. Badilika is not yet on Google Play.

For an update, install over your existing Badilika app. Keep the same package and signing key. Do not uninstall or clear storage if you want to retain settings and history.

02

Connect protection.

  1. Open Protect and enable the categories you want.
  2. Accept Android’s VPN permission and check that protection is connected.
  3. Turn off system Private DNS and browser Secure DNS so requests reach the filter.
  4. Choose each category’s disable delay: at least one full day, up to 30 days.

Another VPN cannot run alongside Badilika. Reopen browsers to close old connections, and test the result on your phone. The app cannot flush another app’s cache or guarantee existing connections are blocked.

03

Set your first app timer.

  1. Open App timers → Choose an app.
  2. Tap the daily allowance clock. Choose hours, then minutes, as you would with an alarm.
  3. 00:30 means 30 minutes per day, not a time of day. Save your timer.
  4. For a repeating allowance, turn on Repeat every few hours and set the window length. For example, choose 30 minutes of allowed time and a 5-hour window. The first window starts when you save.

Daily timers count earlier usage today and reset at local midnight. Repeating timers count usage in the current window. Choose a cooldown between one minute and 30 days for changes. Optional minimum goals send reminders.

04

Enable automatic closing.

Grant Usage access, then open Enable automatic closing and enable Badilika’s Accessibility service in Android settings. Some phones require App info → menu → Allow restricted settings for a sideloaded app.

At the daily limit, Badilika returns the selected app to Home. It does not force-stop its process, and background audio/downloads may continue. Allow background activity if your phone stops the service.

05

Keep a longer view.

Start tracking to save daily history and receive reminders. Grant notification permission when asked. Open Insights to explore usage and data by date. Keep tracking enabled for long-term records; uninstalling or clearing storage deletes local history.
