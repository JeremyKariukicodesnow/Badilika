TOOLS FOR A MORE INTENTIONAL DAY

Small tools.
Meaningful boundaries.

Choose the support that suits you: less time in an app, distance from adult websites, or a better understanding of your digital day.

Porn domain protection

Local adult-domain rules, subdomain matching and Cloudflare Family DNS help filter dedicated adult websites. Explore porn protection ↗

Escort domain protection

A separate curated list targets escort and adult-classified domains. Enable it independently and give it its own disable delay. Explore escort protection ↗

Daily app timers

Choose hours and minutes on a clock. Usage adds up across sessions, and selected apps return to Home when the allowance is used. Set your first timer ↗

Time to reconsider

Wait before increasing or removing a limit or switching off a protection category. Reducing an allowance is immediate. Read the cooldown rules ↗

Private usage insights

Review app time, phone activity and Wi-Fi/mobile data. Filters help you explore different dates. Keep tracking enabled for a longer journal.

A quieter charging screen

An optional analog clock runs as an Android screen saver while charging and idle on supported phones. Find it in the app’s clock tools.

FREE, OPEN AND PERSONAL

Built to help. Free to use.

No ads, accounts or sale of personal data. Usage records stay on your phone. DNS filtering and website hosting use external services, described in the privacy policy.
