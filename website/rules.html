HOW YOUR BOUNDARIES WORK

Clear rules.
Fewer surprises.

Know what happens when you set, change or remove a limit, and which controls belong to Android.

App timer changes.

Reduce an allowanceApplies immediately.
Increase, disable or remove itRequires a completed cooldown, then confirmation.
Shorten its cooldownRequires the current wait to finish.
Pause automatic closing in-appEvery limited app must complete its cooldown.

Protection disable delays.

Porn and escort protection have separate cooldowns, from one full day up to 30 full days. Protection stays active during a pending request and until you explicitly confirm turning it off. Cancelling keeps protection; a later request starts a new full wait.

The countdown consumes monotonic elapsed device time. Powered-off time does not count; rebooting resumes from the persisted remaining amount. Changing the calendar date does not accelerate this countdown. Daily app allowances still use local dates, so clock or timezone changes can affect usage reporting.

Android remains in control.

Revoking VPN, Usage access or Accessibility permissions, switching VPNs, force-stopping, clearing data or uninstalling can defeat safeguards. Badilika does not lock Android settings or guarantee prevention of uninstalling.

Filtering and reporting limits.

Lists can miss domains or misclassify them. DNS protection cannot inspect content inside ordinary apps or reliably prevent arbitrary proxies, VPNs, encrypted DNS or cached connections. Usage and network figures depend on Android records and may be delayed or incomplete; exact router/provider breakdowns are unavailable.
