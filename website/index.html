Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can I change a timer?

You can reduce an allowance immediately. Increasing or removing it requires the selected cooldown.

Does it block every explicit site?

No. Domain filtering can miss sites and cannot inspect content inside other apps. Other VPNs, proxies and encrypted DNS can bypass it.

Where is my data?

Usage history is stored on your phone. DNS queries are sent to Cloudflare when protection is active; the app may download a domain list from GitHub.

Can I turn protection off?

Yes. Request a change, wait for the category's cooldown and confirm. Android settings can revoke permissions or uninstall the app.
