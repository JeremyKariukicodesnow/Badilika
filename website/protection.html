YOUR INTENTIONS DESERVE SOME SPACE

A pause from porn.
A choice for yourself.

Voluntary porn and escort domain protection, with separate controls and time to reconsider before switching either one off.

01 / PORN PROTECTION

Make unwanted sites
less accessible.

Badilika checks domain requests against its local adult list, including subdomains. With porn protection enabled, other queries use Cloudflare Family DNS at 1.1.1.3. A validated list update can be downloaded, with the bundled list available offline.

The goal is dedicated adult domains, rather than blanket restrictions on entertainment or everyday social apps. Classification is imperfect; we cannot promise zero false positives.

YOUR NEXT CHOICE MATTERS

Give yourself
a little room.

02 / ESCORT PROTECTION

A separate filter.
Your own choice.

The curated escort list targets known escort and adult-classified domains, including their subdomains. It is separate from porn protection and is not a complete directory of every service.

CHOOSE

Enable either category.

Open Protect and enable porn protection, escort protection, or both. Accept Android’s VPN permission.

CONNECT

Check the connection.

Protection needs its local VPN connected. Turn off Private DNS and browser Secure DNS. Only one Android VPN can run at a time.

RECONSIDER

Wait before switching off.

Select a 1–30 day disable delay. Request a change, wait, then confirm. Cancel the request anytime to keep protection.

CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Know the limits.

Domain filtering cannot inspect messages, images or videos within general-purpose apps. New sites, encrypted DNS, direct-IP traffic, proxies, another VPN and cached connections may bypass filtering. Badilika does not guarantee that all explicit content is blocked.

Android can revoke permissions, force-stop or uninstall the app. The cooldown is an in-app safeguard, not a system uninstall lock. Do not enable “Block connections without VPN” for this DNS-only implementation.
