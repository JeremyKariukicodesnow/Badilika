Privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026. Badilika is a free, open-source wellbeing tool maintained by Jeremy Kariuki. We do not sell personal data, run advertising, or include app analytics SDKs.

On your phone

Settings, app usage and history are stored locally in the app's private storage. Usage access provides time and data reports. Accessibility observes app/window changes to return selected apps to Home; it is not configured to read window text, passwords or messages. There is no Badilika account or developer-operated collection of your usage journal. CSV exports are shared only when you choose to export them. Uninstalling or clearing storage deletes local records; Android backups are disabled for the app.

External services

Protection sends domain queries to Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.3 for adult protection, otherwise 1.1.1.1). Domain-list updates are downloaded from GitHub. These services can receive your IP address and request information under their own policies. Badilika does not maintain a browsing-domain log.

This website is hosted by Vercel, which may process IP addresses and request logs to serve and secure the website and downloads. This site has no added advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Downloading the app or source code does not send your phone usage history to us.

Your choices

You can skip setup, choose features, revoke Android permissions, export your history or delete the app and its local data. Android controls system permissions. This policy may change; revised policies will show their effective date. Review the source code for implementation details. This policy describes this release, not independently modified forks or third-party services.
